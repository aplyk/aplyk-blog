ABOUT APLYK

Connecting companies and professionals when they need each other.

Aplyk brings companies and professionals together through a more agile, accessible and transparent approach to temporary work.

WHY WE CREATED APLYK

A response to a changing world of work.

The world of work is changing. Economic pressures are leading some companies to reduce their permanent workforce or adopt more flexible staffing models, while technologies such as artificial intelligence are transforming how businesses operate.

At the same time, professionals face rising everyday living costs and may need additional sources of income. Yet opportunities do not always reach the people who are available to take them. Companies can struggle to fill vacancies even when skilled professionals are ready to work.

We recognised a substantial gap between these two realities: businesses needed timely access to talent, and professionals needed a straightforward way to offer their services. What was missing was a platform capable of bringing both sides together efficiently.

Drawing on our founder’s experience working with a similar platform in the United Kingdom, we created Aplyk to help bridge that gap. Our ambition is to democratise access to temporary work: helping companies respond more flexibly, manage costs and remain competitive, while enabling professionals to access opportunities and earn additional income.

OUR MISSION

Transparent connections, in real time.

Enable transparent, real-time connections between companies and temporary professionals. We aim to make opportunities more accessible and help businesses find the people they need without unnecessary complexity.

OUR VISION

The infrastructure for modern temporary hiring.

Become a reference platform for temporary hiring, helping businesses adapt to more flexible, on-demand staffing while giving professionals greater access to work opportunities.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions about temporary work with Aplyk

What is temporary work?expand_more
Temporary work is work carried out for a defined period or to cover a specific business need. Aplyk helps professionals discover temporary shift opportunities published by companies; Aplyk is not the employer.
How can I find jobs near me?expand_more
Add your location and availability to your profile. Aplyk is designed to surface relevant opportunities based on your area, skills and available times.
Can I find hourly work?expand_more
Yes. Companies can publish hourly and shift-based opportunities. Availability depends on company demand and how well each opportunity matches your profile.
Can I find weekend work?expand_more
Weekend opportunities may be available when companies publish shifts for those days. You can set your availability so suitable weekend work can be prioritised.
What types of jobs can I find on Aplyk?expand_more
Aplyk focuses on shift-based opportunities in sectors such as hospitality, logistics and warehousing, including roles such as waiter/waitress, kitchen assistant, warehouse operative, picker, packer and forklift operator.
Is Aplyk a temporary staffing agency?expand_more
No. Aplyk is a technology platform that connects companies with professionals for temporary work opportunities. The hiring company remains responsible for employment, payroll and its legal obligations.
How do I receive new shifts?expand_more
When compatible opportunities are available, Aplyk can show them and send alerts based on your profile, location, skills and availability.
Can I reject an opportunity?expand_more
Yes. You can review an opportunity before accepting it and decide whether it suits you. You are not required to accept every shift shown to you.

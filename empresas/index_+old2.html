QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions about hiring temporary staff

How do I hire temporary staff with Aplyk?expand_more
Create a shift with the role, location, schedule and requirements. Aplyk helps identify compatible professionals so your company can review the available information and make the final decision.
How does Aplyk find available professionals?expand_more
Aplyk uses information such as availability, location, skills and previous reliability or performance to help prioritise professionals for the specific shift.
Is Aplyk a temporary staffing agency?expand_more
No. Aplyk is a technology platform. It connects businesses and professionals and supports the digital workflow, but it does not act as the legal employer.
Who employs the professional?expand_more
The hiring company maintains the employment relationship and remains responsible for the contract, payroll and applicable statutory obligations.
How does matching work?expand_more
Matching considers the requirements of the shift together with relevant professional information such as availability, location, skills and previous performance.
What does it mean for a professional to be validated?expand_more
Validation can include identity, right-to-work and relevant skill information where applicable, helping the company review clearer information before confirming a professional.
Can I see a professional's performance history?expand_more
Where previous Aplyk activity exists, the platform can present relevant information such as completed shifts, attendance, ratings and previous performance to support the next decision.
Can I use Aplyk for a single shift?expand_more
Yes. Aplyk can support one-off temporary staffing needs as well as recurring shift requirements.
Can I create a pool of regular professionals?expand_more
Yes. Previous performance, reliability, availability and company preference can help you build a reusable pool of professionals you already know.
How is attendance tracked?expand_more
Aplyk provides operational tools for managing attendance and time worked so the company can keep a clearer record of each shift.
Which industries can use Aplyk?expand_more
Aplyk is designed for shift-based operations including hospitality, logistics, warehousing and agriculture, and can also support other businesses with similar temporary staffing requirements.

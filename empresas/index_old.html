QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions about hiring temporary staff

How do I hire temporary staff with Aplyk?expand_more
Create and publish a shift with the role, location, schedule and requirements. Aplyk helps you identify compatible professionals so you can review and confirm suitable people.
Is Aplyk a temporary staffing agency?expand_more
No. Aplyk is a technology platform that connects businesses with professionals and provides tools to manage temporary shifts.
Who employs the professional?expand_more
The hiring company maintains the employment relationship and is responsible for the contract, payroll and applicable legal obligations.
How does matching work?expand_more
Aplyk uses information such as skills, availability, location and shift requirements to help identify professionals who may be compatible with the role.
Can I use Aplyk to cover a single shift?expand_more
Yes. Aplyk can be used for one-off shifts as well as recurring temporary staffing needs.
Which sectors does Aplyk cover?expand_more
Aplyk is designed for shift-based sectors such as hospitality, logistics, warehousing and agriculture, as well as other businesses with similar temporary staffing needs.
How does time tracking work?expand_more
The platform provides time-tracking tools so the company can manage attendance and the hours associated with each shift.
How do ratings work?expand_more
After completed work, ratings can contribute to a clearer record of previous performance and help businesses build a trusted professional pool.
Can I create a pool of regular professionals?expand_more
Yes. Previous performance, ratings and shift history can help you build a pool of professionals you already know and may want to work with again.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Aplyk uses cookies, local storage and similar technologies on aplyk.com. The current homepage does not load third-party analytics or advertising trackers. The consent manager is nevertheless designed to keep those categories disabled unless and until a visitor authorises them.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small files or identifiers that a website can store on, or read from, your device. Similar technologies, including localStorage, can also retain information in your browser. Consent rules may apply to both.

3. Consent categories

Strictly necessary

Always active. Used for essential functions and to remember your privacy choice.

Preferences

Allows optional choices, such as the website language, to be remembered.

Analytics

Would be used to measure website usage. The current Aplyk homepage does not load an analytics tracker.

Marketing

Would be used for advertising or campaign measurement. The current Aplyk homepage does not load a marketing tracker.

4. How to manage or withdraw consent

On your first visit you can accept all optional technologies, reject them, or configure each category. You can later reopen the panel through the “Cookie Settings” link in the footer. Withdrawing consent disables future use of the relevant category, and Aplyk removes its own local preference storage where it should no longer be retained.

5. Third-party services

The website may request technical resources from external providers, for example web fonts, and the demo form is submitted through FormSubmit when you choose to send it. These services are not described here as analytics or marketing cookies because the current homepage does not load such trackers. If Aplyk introduces new storage or tracking technologies, this policy and the consent manager must be updated before they are enabled where consent is required.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or Aplyk’s use of storage technologies, contact info@aplyk.com.

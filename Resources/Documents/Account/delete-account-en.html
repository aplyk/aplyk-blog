Delete your Aplyk account

1

Delete it in the app

Open Aplyk and go to Settings → Delete account.

2

Delete it without the app

If you can’t use the app or sign in, request deletion here. We’ll email a link to your account’s address to confirm it’s you. The link expires in 1 hour.

Request account deletion
3

What we delete

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, photo, address, availability and work preferences.
  • Your work history, languages, certificates and the documents you uploaded.
  • Your identity verification records.
  • Your shift applications, bookings, clock-in and clock-out records, and the ratings you gave and received.
  • Your sign-in details, devices, notifications and settings, and your place in any company team.
4

What we keep

  • Company records stay with the company, without your name: shifts, locations and company documents.
  • A minimal record of the deletion, so it can be applied again if a backup is ever restored.
  • Records the law requires us to keep, only for as long as it requires.
  • Backups and our identity verification provider may hold copies for a limited time after deletion.
5

How long it takes

If nothing needs to be settled first, your account is deleted straight away. If you have upcoming shifts, hours still to be confirmed, or you’re the only owner of a company, our team reviews the request and confirms by email within one month.
